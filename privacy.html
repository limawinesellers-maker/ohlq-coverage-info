Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

OHLQ Coverage Automation is a private internal tool operated by Lima Wine Sellers.

Information accessed

With the account owner’s authorization, the application uses Google Drive access to place the daily OHLQ Item Coverage CSV report into a designated folder. Authentication credentials and tokens are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets.

Use of information

Google user data is used only to upload and, when needed, replace the dated report file in the designated Google Drive folder. The application does not sell, advertise with, or share Google user data with third parties.

Retention and control

Uploaded reports remain in the account owner’s Google Drive and are controlled by that owner. Access may be revoked at any time through the Google Account security settings. Removing the authorization stops future Drive access.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests may be sent to limawinesellers@gmail.com.
